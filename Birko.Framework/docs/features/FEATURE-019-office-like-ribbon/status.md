## What can be tested now

The layout fix: in the Birko.Web Playground, every ribbon group is now one height, commands line up in rows across groups, and each group's name sits underneath it. Please look at it in light and dark and at a narrow window.

Arrow keys: Tab into the ribbon, press the down arrow, then move between commands and groups with the arrow keys; a folded group opens with Enter. Please also try it with a screen reader (NVDA or Narrator).

Menus: in the Playground ribbon, "Paste" has a small arrow that opens more paste options, and "CSV" opens a menu; try them with the mouse and the keyboard, and on a narrow window.

Tooltips: rest the pointer on "Cut" or "Paste" in the Playground ribbon; a light card shows its name, shortcut and a short description. Moving to the next command shows its tooltip at once, and reaching a command with the keyboard shows it too.

On/off buttons: in the Playground ribbon, "Bold" and "Italic" switch on and off, and "Columns" / "Rows" form a pair where only one can be on.

Collapsing: double-click a ribbon tab, or press Ctrl+F1 anywhere in the app, to hide the ribbon down to its tabs; do it again to bring it back.

Quick Access Toolbar: Save, Undo and Redo sit before the ribbon tabs in the Playground. Right-click a ribbon command to add it there, right-click a toolbar button to remove it, and use the small arrow to show, hide or reset; the choice is remembered after a reload.
